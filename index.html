FAQ

Questions about giving.

Is my donation secure?

Yes. Donations are processed through Stripe, so your card details never touch our site.

Can I give any amount?

Every amount helps put a toy in a kid's hands. Choose what works for you on the checkout page.

Can I donate toys instead of money?

Email us at info@thel4wfoundation.com and we'll let you know where and when to drop them off.

Who runs the toy drive?

The L4W Foundation, founded by Lawrence Butler to create pathways for youth from Southwest Atlanta through baseball, financial education, and mentorship.
